# Jubilee Superstore Sales Australia (2022–2025)

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, and business trends for Jubilee Superstore Australia between 2022 and 2025.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends, evaluate regional and product performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of sales data, making it challenging to identify performance trends and profitability drivers.

This project aims to provide management with a centralized dashboard to:

* Monitor sales performance
* Track profitability metrics
* Identify top-performing products and regions
* Support strategic business decisions
* Improve operational efficiency

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains transactional sales records from 2022 to 2025, including:

* Order Date
* Product Category
* Product Sub-Category
* Sales Revenue
* Profit

* Region
* State
* Quantity Sold

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Gross Profit Margin (GPM)

Measures the percentage of revenue retained after accounting for the cost of goods sold.

### Operating Profit Margin (OPM)

Measures operational efficiency by evaluating operating profit relative to revenue.

### Additional Metrics

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Quantity Sold
* Year-over-Year Growth
* Regional Performance
